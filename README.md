# Kahreez

**Enterprise AI transformation framework and product design covering discovery, use-case prioritization, ROI assessment, and adoption planning.**

Kahreez is a framework and product design for AI transformation decisions. It helps consultants and enterprise teams decide what to change before they automate. It takes a business problem through discovery, process mapping, and root-cause analysis to an evidence-linked recommendation: which intervention to use, whether the current environment can support it, and how to pilot and adopt it.

> This repository is a portfolio overview. It has documentation only. Internal design documents and any implementation work are private and not published here.

## Business problem

Many AI initiatives begin with a technology choice instead of a diagnosed problem. That leads to pilots that automate the wrong process, ignore simpler fixes, or stall because the underlying systems and data cannot support them. Teams need a structured way to understand the problem first, compare AI and non-AI options fairly, and justify the chosen path with evidence.

**Intended users:** AI transformation consultants, enterprise AI teams, systems integrators, solution architects, and modernization teams.

## Design work completed

- **Transformation methodology:** a decision lifecycle that runs from discovery and root-cause diagnosis, through intervention choice, readiness, and modernization, to pilot, adoption, handoff, and outcome measurement.
- **Product design and roadmap:** a finalized product and development roadmap that defines the core decision engines, the case and evidence model, the user experience, and readiness criteria for a pilot.
- **Multi-surface product architecture:** one shared decision core serving a web application, a ChatGPT app, and a Claude Code plugin, all using the same decision, evidence, and reporting models.
- **Consulting fieldbook:** a practical fieldbook for running AI transformation engagements, covering discovery, prioritization, readiness, architecture advisory, governance, business cases, pilots, and handoff.

## Designed capabilities

The capabilities below come from the product design. This overview describes the design only and does not present Kahreez as a released product.

- **Business discovery:** guided discovery that captures the business outcome, the current process, and the evidence behind each finding.
- **Process mapping and root-cause analysis:** map how the current result is produced and identify root causes before choosing a solution.
- **Intervention selection:** compare a full range of options, including no change, process redesign, conventional software, rules and workflow automation, traditional analytics, generative AI, retrieval-based AI, agentic AI, and human-led approaches.
- **Readiness and modernization assessment:** check whether the current technology, data, and organization can safely support an intervention, and identify the minimum modernization it requires.
- **Use-case prioritization and ROI:** rank use cases and compare value, risk, and total cost of ownership.
- **Pilot, evaluation, and adoption planning:** design pilots with clear evaluation criteria, then plan governance, adoption, and handoff.
- **Evidence-linked reporting:** recommendations that show the evidence behind them and what new evidence would change them.

## AI capabilities and human-review boundaries

AI features in the product design:

- AI assistance with discovery, such as suggesting follow-up questions and summarizing evidence.
- Structured AI outputs that are validated before use.
- Per-engagement budgets for AI usage costs.

Human-review boundaries in the design:

- **AI is never the default recommendation.** Every AI option is compared with simpler process, software, and operating-model alternatives.
- Recommendations are linked to evidence and stay open to challenge.
- A person must confirm consequential actions and final recommendations.
- Kahreez is not designed as an autonomous agent, a generic chatbot, or an automated code-migration tool.

## How it works

```text
Discover → Map process → Diagnose root cause → Compare interventions
→ Assess readiness and modernization → Prioritize → Evaluate value, risk, and cost
→ Design pilot → Govern and adopt → Roadmap and handoff → Measure outcomes
```

1. Capture the business outcome and how the current process works.
2. Diagnose the root cause before considering solutions.
3. Compare AI and non-AI interventions.
4. Check whether the environment can support the chosen option, and define the minimum modernization it needs.
5. Prioritize use cases by value, risk, and cost.
6. Design a pilot, then plan adoption, governance, and handoff.
